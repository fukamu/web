## About this document

This pre-release draft explains how FUKAMU株式会社 handles information on its corporate website. Separate official documents apply to information handled within each product.

## Information in email inquiries

When you contact us by email, we may receive your name, email address, organization, message, and any other information you choose to include. This draft proposes using that information to review and answer the inquiry, make necessary contact, maintain response records, and improve our products and operations.

Please do not include unnecessary personal or sensitive information, or private product content, in an email inquiry.

## Retention, deletion, and service providers

We intend to retain inquiry information only as long as needed to respond and maintain appropriate records. After the purpose is fulfilled, we intend to delete it unless retention is necessary for legal obligations, dispute handling, security, or similar reasons. The specific retention period, email provider, service providers, and any overseas handling will be confirmed against actual operations before publication.

We will not provide personal data to a third party without consent except where permitted by law. If processing is entrusted to a service provider, we intend to select and oversee that provider appropriately and limit processing to what is necessary.

## Security and individual requests

We intend to use security measures appropriate to the nature and scope of the information handled. Identity verification, procedures, and scope for requests concerning retained personal data—including notification of purpose, disclosure, correction, and cessation of use—will be aligned with actual operations before publication.

The public email address on the Contact page will be the point of contact for privacy inquiries and individual requests.

## Revisions

We will review this document when our practices or applicable requirements change. The effective date will be added at publication, and the Japanese version and this English reference translation will be updated together.
